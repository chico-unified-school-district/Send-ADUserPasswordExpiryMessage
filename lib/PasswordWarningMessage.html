Password requirements

You must change your password at least once every six months. Your new password must be at least 12 characters long; 16 characters is recommended.

Include at least 3 of these 4 character types:

  • Uppercase letters (A-Z)
  • Lowercase letters (a-z)
  • Numbers (0-9)
  • Special characters (such as !, @, or #)

How to change your password

The quickest option is to use the online password reset tool:

Open the Password Reset Tool

Or, from a district Windows computer:

  1. Sign in and press CTRL+ALT+DEL.
  2. Select Change a Password and follow the prompts.
  3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice to confirm it.
